# Out-of-sample backtest: 2026-06-01 → 2026-08-20 (Unusual Whales replay)

First statistically meaningful test of the live engine. `strategy-engine/uw_backtest.py`
replayed 55 sessions minute-by-minute through the real `build_signal` (all three lanes,
live per-lane family policy, live executor caps of 2 trades/day, wall setups on the
~3DTE chain, live premium stops 35%/20%, fills crossing a modeled spread).

## Headline

**93 trades · 40% win rate · profit factor 0.97 · expectancy −$0.78 ± $6.80 SE per
contract, gross of commissions.** Statistically indistinguishable from zero gross;
slightly negative net of ~$1.50–3 round-trip fees. The system is neither an edge nor
a disaster: it is cost-line breakeven with one clearly bleeding component.

- **GEX_WALL_BOUNCE is the only component ~3 SE below zero** — 1 win in 8, and the
  losses are premium-stops and all-day bleeds. Buying the bounce at a wall looks
  structurally wrong on this sample; the other two wall reactions (rejection,
  failed-break) are fine-to-positive.
- Excluding BOUNCE + ORB_INDEX, the rest runs **+$6.21 ± 6.71/trade (n=81)** — still
  under 1 SE from zero. No family demonstrates a statistically positive edge yet.

## Fidelity caveats (all documented in uw_backtest.py)

More permissive than live: no ZeroGEX decision blockers. Less favorable than live:
modeled spreads on candle mids (no NBBO), stop-first intrabar sequencing, thin-candle
forward-fill on multi-day contracts. GEX comes from UW's dealer model, not ZeroGEX.
Gross of commissions throughout.

## Suggested next experiments (in order of information value)

1. Extend the window back 6+ months (UW has ≥1 year) — doubles the sample and adds
   regimes; the June→August decay needs more data to read.
2. Scenario: disable GEX_WALL_BOUNCE (keep rejection/break-fail) and re-run — the
   one change this sample actually supports testing.
3. Scenario: entries before 11:00 ET only.
4. Cross-validate one recorded live day's ZeroGEX flip/walls against UW-derived
   values to size the GEX-model disagreement.
