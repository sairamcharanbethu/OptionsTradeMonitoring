## Decision 1 — GEX_WALL_BOUNCE disabled ✅ (shipped)

Baseline bounce trades: **n=12, 17% win, mean −$40.42 ± $13.08 SE — ~3.1 SE
below zero**, consistent with the June–August sub-sample measured first
(−$40.99 ± 14.48, n=8; ~+4 independent April–May trades confirmed rather than
reverted). It is the single difference between portfolio PF 0.88 and 1.03.

Mechanical story: the wall gate arms only in strong negative gamma — exactly
the regime where walls break rather than hold. A bounce entry fights the
condition its own gate selects for. The other two wall reactions trade *with*
that logic and were positive on the same sample (rejection +$390.90 on 8,
failed-break +$151.10 on 13).

Shipped as `DISABLED_WALL_STRATEGIES = {"GEX_WALL_BOUNCE"}` in
signal_engine.py (candidate never arms; evaluator unchanged), pinned by
test_wall_strategy_policy.py. Re-enable only via a fresh uw_backtest run.

## Decision 2 — morning-only entry gate REJECTED ❌ (evidence reversed)

The June–August sample suggested entries after 11:00 ET were poison. The full
window says otherwise: morning_only *underperformed* baseline (−$3.49 vs
−$3.03), and baseline's 11:xx bucket was actually positive (+$6.37/trade,
n=18). The 93-trade pattern was noise. **This is the system working as
intended — a change that looked obvious two weeks ago failed out-of-sample
before touching the engine.**

## Honest bottom line

Removing the proven bleeder makes the system **breakeven-to-slightly-positive
gross (+$0.64/trade, PF 1.03) and still ~flat-to-negative net of ~$1.50–3
round-trip commissions.** This change stops a leak; it does not yet establish
an edge. The positive families (CONTINUATION +$9.37 ± 9.84 n=29,
GEX_WALL_REJECTION, GEX_WALL_BREAK_FAIL) are each under ~1.2 SE from zero —
promising, unproven. The evidence loop continues: the UW window rolls forward
daily, so each week adds ~5 fresh out-of-sample sessions to re-test against.
